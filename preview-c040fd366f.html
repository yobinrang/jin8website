Our Story

Rooted in 晋(jìn)
the heart of Shanxi.

For centuries the merchant clans of Shanxi — the Jìn Shāng — moved silk, coal, tea and culture along China's oldest trade routes. They were patient people, who believed that anything worth making was worth making slowly.

Jin 8 carries that spirit south to Box Hill. Every cocktail draws from Shanxi's living traditions — the aged vinegar of Qingxu, the mellow fire of Fén Jiǔ, the millet and honey of the loess plateau — poured one careful glass at a time.
