Our Story

Rooted in 晋(jìn)
the heart of Shanxi.

For centuries the merchant clans of Shanxi — the Jìn Shāng — moved silk, coal, tea and culture along China's oldest trade routes. They were patient people, who believed that anything worth making was worth making slowly.

Jin 8 carries that spirit to Box Hill. Our signature cocktails start with fenjiu, the clear spirit Shanxi has distilled for centuries, and many take their names from its mountains, old rituals and family customs. Ask the team and they'll tell you the story behind each one.
